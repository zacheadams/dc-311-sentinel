References & Acknowledgements

This project was built in a Ghostty terminal using Antigravity-CLI running Gemini 4 Hydrogen, and is hosted on GitHub. The basemaps are Stamen Toner, rendered in Leaflet.js, with other graphics using Chart.js, data stored in DuckDB, and exports facilitated using jsPDF. The data used are publicly available thanks to Open Data DC. This project builds on and generalizes the work of Missed Collections, and was spurred on by a conversation with Councilmember-elect Oye Owolewa, who asked how an elected official could tell, beyond vocal civic engagement and reports from agencies, what problems were emergent and unaddressed in the community.
